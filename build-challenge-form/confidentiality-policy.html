1. Purpose of information use

You are submitting information about your product, MVP, architecture, and related materials for the sole purpose of allowing HyKr and its evaluation panel to review your application for the HyKr Build Challenge and to make related internal program decisions.

HyKr will use the information and materials you provide only for:

  • Evaluating your MVP and technical progress.
  • Conducting internal discussions about your application and fit for the program.
  • Contacting you regarding the challenge, including clarifications, shortlisting, next steps, or feedback, where applicable.

HyKr does not seek any ownership of your intellectual property through this submission. All IP in your product, code, designs, documentation, and related materials remains with you or your company, subject to any separate written agreements you may sign with HyKr in the future.

HyKr intends to treat your Submission as confidential and use it only within the scope described above. We will not deliberately publish or disclose your Submission to the general public.

  • HyKr team members directly involved in the challenge and its operations.

HyKr will not share your detailed Submission with third parties for unrelated commercial purposes (for example, selling your data) without your prior consent, except:

You are responsible for ensuring that any links you share (e.g., GitHub, Notion, Drive, Loom) have appropriate access settings and do not unintentionally expose sensitive information beyond what you are comfortable sharing for evaluation.

If you are not comfortable sharing certain details (e.g., full repo access, sensitive implementation details), you may instead provide high-level overviews or redacted information that still allows for a fair technical evaluation.
